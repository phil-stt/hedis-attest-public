With one reviewer there is no statistical test here. The layer is instrumented so that when several nurses work in parallel, assignment, exposure, and decisions are already recorded per arm. The rate that decides between arms is the QA-audited error rate: a sample of accepted findings re-reviewed by a second person on the QA page, overturned over audited. A "catch" is a rejection of a proposed finding; it is confirmed when QA upholds it. Decisions per hour is context, never the score.
